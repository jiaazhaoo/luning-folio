Mobility data products for UK cities

Privacy-preserving, research-ready datasets derived from smartphone location data

Urban Big Data Centre, University of Glasgow

Raw smartphone GPS data cannot be shared, mainly because of privacy concerns. At UBDC we validate these data and derive aggregated, shareable products that other researchers can use for transport, planning and public-health studies.

  • H3 Mobile Mobility Dataset for 55 major UK cities (2017–2024). Location observations indexed to Uber’s H3 hexagonal grid at resolution 13 and linked to UK administrative geographies.
  • Home-location dataset for 55 major UK cities. Yearly home-area estimates for anonymised app users at LSOA (England) and Data Zone (Scotland) level, linked to IMD 2019 and SIMD 2020 deprivation indicators.

These products support the park-use research and wider UBDC work on transport and mobility.
