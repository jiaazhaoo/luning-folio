Who uses city parks, measured with mobile phone app data

  • Park visit estimates from mobile phone app data, Glasgow City Region
  • Bias checks and weighting against the population
  • Demand for park quality across 486 parks in five UK cities

Outputs

  1. GISRUK
    Building Scalable Nature-Use Indicators for Thriving Cities: Modelling Demand for Park Quality Using Mobile Phone App Data
    Luning Li, Jess Hepburn, Fiona Caryl, Jonathan Olsen, Richard Mitchell, Qunshan Zhao, and 2 more authors
    In 34th Annual GIS Research UK Conference (GISRUK 2026), 2026
    Abs DOI Bib HTML PDF

    Using a multi-city UK sample of 486 parks across Birmingham, Glasgow, Edinburgh, Manchester and Bristol, this paper pairs mobile phone app data with park quality metrics to model demand for urban parks, finding that park quality explains substantial variation in visitation.

  2. GISRUK
    Mobile Phone App Data for Understanding Urban Public Park Usage: Opportunities and Challenges
    Luning Li and Michael Sinclair
    In 33rd Annual GIS Research UK Conference (GISRUK 2025), 2025
    Abs DOI Bib HTML PDF

    Using two independent mobile phone app datasets covering three years in the Glasgow City Region, we build a framework to estimate park visitation and map park usage across socio-demographic groups. Preliminary findings highlight the potential of mobile phone app data for providing scalable, highly granular insights into park usage, while also revealing inherent challenges.
