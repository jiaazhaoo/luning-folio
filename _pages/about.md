I am a quantitative human geographer and GIScientist. I study **human dynamics in urban environments using geospatial big data**: where people go, how long they stay, and what the places around them mean for their health, wellbeing and exposure to harm.

Since May 2024 I have been a Research Associate in Urban Analytics at the [Urban Big Data Centre (UBDC)](https://www.ubdc.ac.uk/), University of Glasgow. There I work on an ESRC-funded project that uses **mobile phone app data to understand how people use urban parks and green spaces** across UK cities. The aim is scalable nature-use indicators that can inform urban planning and public health.

Before Glasgow, I was a Research Associate in Quantitative Human Geography in the Quantitative Spatial Science (QuSS) group at the [University of Bristol](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/geography/), funded by the [Bristol Hub for Gambling Harms Research](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/research/groups/gambling-harms/). That work built the first longitudinal dataset of gambling shops in Great Britain and examined how exposure to physical gambling environments shapes gambling behaviour, including online gambling.

I received my PhD in GIScience (2023) and MSc in Natural Resources (2019) from [Beijing Normal University](https://www.bnu.edu.cn/), and my BSc in GIScience, with a minor in Applied Psychology, from [East China Normal University](https://www.ecnu.edu.cn/) (2016). My doctoral research used **Wi-Fi probe data** to study crowds and tourist behaviour in open public places.

**Research interests**

- Human mobility and urban analytics with mobile phone, GPS and Wi-Fi data
- Urban green space use, nature-based wellbeing and public health
- Geographies of gambling and place-based gambling harms
- Tourist behaviour and crowd dynamics in open public places
- Spatial data science, causal inference and machine learning for geography

You can also find me on the [University of Glasgow staff pages](https://www.gla.ac.uk/schools/socialpolitical/staff/luningli/), at [UBDC](https://www.ubdc.ac.uk/author/luning-li), and in the [Enlighten repository](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/view/author/77267.html).
