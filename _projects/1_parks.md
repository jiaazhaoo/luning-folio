**Urban Big Data Centre, University of Glasgow · 2024–present · ESRC-funded**

Urban parks matter for health, wellbeing and social life, yet we know surprisingly little about who actually visits them, when, and why. Traditional visitor surveys are costly, infrequent and hard to compare across cities.

This project uses large-scale, anonymised **mobile phone app (MPA) location data** to estimate park visitation across UK cities. It covers:

- a framework for **estimating park visits** and mapping use across socio-demographic groups, built on two independent app datasets covering three years in the Glasgow City Region;
- **representativeness and weighting**, to see how far app users stand in for the wider population and how to correct for bias;
- **nature-use indicators** for 486 parks across Birmingham, Glasgow, Edinburgh, Manchester and Bristol, which link visitation to park quality.

The project is part of UBDC's programme on [understanding the use of urban greenspaces through big data](https://www.ubdc.ac.uk/research-theme/urban-sustainability/understanding-use-of-urban-greenspaces-with-big-data).
